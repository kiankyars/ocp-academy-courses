Welcome to module 7: Control sequences and operation. Make operating states and responsibilities explicit.

Operating logic needs explicit states, permissives, failure responses and ownership. Automated commands do not replace independent verification of a safe state.

We will work through five connected questions, then apply the central ideas in a short knowledge check. The paper links under the teaching slides take you to the relevant pages, so you can examine the underlying discussion while keeping the course's system boundary in view.
