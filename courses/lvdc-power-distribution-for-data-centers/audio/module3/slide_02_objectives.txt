# pronunciation: LVDC = L V D C; OCP = O C P; AC = A C; DC = D C. Technical numbers and abbreviations are written for speech in the body.

This module connects three review tasks. Trace load and fault current paths. compare TN-S and information technology implications. use the configuration matrix to identify trade-offs and missing evidence.

Polarity, midpoint distribution, earthing and voltage form a combined choice. Device compatibility and protection must be checked for that specific combination.

The five teaching slides build those tasks into a single system view. Read the paper links when you need the original discussion and its qualifications. Before the knowledge check, take a moment to apply the ideas to the short review prompt. You can replay the narration, inspect the figures at full size, and revisit a slide as needed.
