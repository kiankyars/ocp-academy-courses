Other assumptions are intentionally incomplete. The source and load voltage envelopes have not yet been agreed. The polarity and earthing choice needs product evidence. The rack entry architecture and its isolation responsibilities must be confirmed. No universal voltage transient or timing value is supplied to fill those gaps.

Begin the review by separating a design assumption from verification evidence. An assumption states what the proposed analysis relies on. Evidence demonstrates whether the actual system can satisfy the intended behavior under the relevant conditions. A clean drawing is useful, but it is not evidence of every behavior it implies.

Use the scenario to practice asking precise questions. Which claim is already supported, which remains conditional, and which is contradicted by the available path? The goal is a review that leads to specific engineering actions rather than an unsupported approval or rejection of L V D C as a whole.
