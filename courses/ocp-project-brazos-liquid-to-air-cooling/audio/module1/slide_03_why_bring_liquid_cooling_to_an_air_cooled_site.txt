# pronunciation: IT -> I T; OCP -> O C P

Brazos addresses a practical deployment problem. The I T equipment needs liquid at its cooling interfaces, while the building already removes heat through air. Replacing the facility cooling distribution can be a substantial project. Brazos introduces a local liquid loop whose heat ultimately returns to that existing air system.

Google introduced Brazos in June twenty twenty-six. The O C P specification used here is version zero point seventy-five point four, effective September eleventh, twenty twenty-six. Its design requirements give us a more detailed basis for review than the original launch description.

The opportunity is incremental deployment, subject to site capacity. An available rack position does not establish sufficient electrical power, acceptable air temperature, or enough air-handling capacity. Those remain engineering questions. Start with a specific I T load and a specific location, then test whether the complete cooling path can support them.
